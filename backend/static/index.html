How Human First works

  1. Crisis screen (no AI). Words like "suicide", "overdose", or "chest pain" immediately show 911, 988, and Poison Control. A model never improvises in an emergency.
  2. Repeat check (no AI). If you ask something you were just redirected on, you see research on AI's costs to people and the planet, then you choose whether to ask AI anyway.
  3. Gatekeeper agent. Sorts the prompt into appropriate, personal, high-stakes, or searchable. It reads your prompt only as data, so "ignore your rules" tricks don't work.
  4. Redirect agent. Only for blocked prompts. Uses tools to find real links (Google Maps, Yelp, care finders, support organizations) and writes a short note pointing you to a person or a better tool.

Where the footprint numbers come from

Anthropic does not publish energy or water use per Claude prompt, so Human First estimates a range:

  • Low, 0.24 Wh and 0.26 mL water: Google's measured median Gemini text prompt (Aug 2025).
  • High, 1.86 Wh: MIT Technology Review's measurement of a large open model, Llama 3.1 405B (6,706 joules per response, May 2025).
  • Central, ~0.67 Wh: the midpoint (geometric mean) of the two. Your totals use this.
  • Water: Google's on-site cooling rate (1.08 mL per Wh), times 4 to include the water used to generate the electricity (about three-quarters of data-center water, per a 2026 Harvard Salata Institute study).
  • Longer prompts cost more: the estimate grows by 1 + (prompt tokens / 2,000).
